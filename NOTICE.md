# Notice: clips belong to their creators

Moments Clipper is a tool. The videos you clip, and the shorts made from them, belong to the people who made them.

- **Only clip creators who allow it.** Many streamers welcome clips; some don't. Check their rules first.
- **Follow campaign terms.** Paid clipping programs have their own rules (credit, hashtags, what not to show). The app helps you follow them, but you are responsible for what you post.
- **Credit the creator** in the caption. The post kit does this for you when a campaign is set.
- **Downloads.** The app uses yt-dlp to read public videos for your personal clipping work. Respect YouTube's and other platforms' terms of service.

The screenshots in this repository show frames from public streams to illustrate the tool; they belong to their creators.
